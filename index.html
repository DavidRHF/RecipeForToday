You don't get to pick.Neither does taste.

Three recipes a day, drawn blind from a pot of thirty. Half of them are genuinely good. Half of them are genuinely not. You find out which after you've eaten it.

The deal

Three rolls. No takebacks.

The rules exist so you can't game your way to the good half. That's the whole product.

Rule 01

Three a day, then the pot's empty

Rolls reset at midnight, your time. Breakfast, lunch, dinner. Spend them how you like, but spend them carefully.

Rule 02

The rating stays under the lid

You get the full recipe, every ingredient and every step. You don't get the star rating until you press "I cooked it." No peeking, no pre-screening.

Rule 03

The odds are exactly even

Every roll flips a coin first. Heads pulls from the recipes rated 2.6 and up. Tails pulls from 2.5 and down. Fifty-fifty, every single time.

Your pantry

Thirty recipes. Locked until you draw them.

Every recipe you roll stays here for good. Cook it and the rating unlocks with it.

On the recipes: all thirty are original, written for this project and released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The bad ones are unappetizing, never unsafe — no undercooked meat, no food-safety roulette. Source on GitHub.
